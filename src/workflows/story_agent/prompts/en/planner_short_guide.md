## IMPORTANT: This is a SHORT story ({story_length})

Guidelines for short stories (unless user requested otherwise):

1. **Characters**: Recommended 0-3 characters to keep it focused.
   - Focus on the main protagonist.
   - Keep character backgrounds brief.

2. **Plot Structure**: Simple and focused.
   - Single clear conflict/problem.
   - Quick resolution.

3. **Outline**: Keep sections brief.
   - Introduction: Immediately introduce character and situation.
   - Conflict: One clear problem.
   - Climax: Quick turning point.
   - Resolution: Swift, satisfying ending.

4. **General**:
   - PRIORITIZE the user's specific instructions in the prompt over these guidelines.
   - If the user asks for "5 paragraphs", maintain the structure but ensure it fits that length.
